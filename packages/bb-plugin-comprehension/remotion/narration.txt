An agent can finish the work and still leave you with a comprehension problem. What changed? Why did it change? What needs your attention?

Comprehension starts with one question. Do you want the whole thread, one message, or a selected passage? That choice defines the context.

The server freezes that context. It keeps the matching thread rows, caps the source, and records the exact point in the conversation. This is a snapshot, not a live feed.

A hidden child thread receives the report skill and Quiet Newsroom template. It turns the snapshot into one H T M L document. The worker writes out of sight, so the explanation does not interrupt you.

The report comes back as a reusable view. It gives you a starting point, a table of contents, open sections, and diagrams. You can read it, reopen it, or embed it in the thread.

But a finished report is not the same as a current briefing. It does not yet show what changed, which decisions were difficult, or what evidence matters.

That is the temporal brief: status, context, change, evidence, and next action. One brief can become H T M L, narrated slides, a player, or a video.
